Help Center & Campus Guide

Frequently asked questions about student buying, selling, and safety on Zimbabwean campuses.

Register a UniTrade student account, choose your university campus, and head to Sell or your Vendor Dashboard. Set your price in US$, choose the condition, write a short description, and your listing goes live instantly!

UniTrade supports direct local Zimbabwean payment options: EcoCash (USD wallet), InnBucks vouchers, OneMoney, ZIPIT bank transfers, and Cash on campus.

Always meet in well-lit, public campus hubs like university libraries, student dining halls, or department foyers. Inspect goods thoroughly before finalizing payment transfers.
