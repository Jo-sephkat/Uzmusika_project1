About UniTrade

UniTrade is the dedicated student marketplace built for Zimbabwean university campuses.

We connect student buyers and sellers across Zimbabwean campuses to trade textbooks, laptops, hostel gear, notes, tutoring sessions, food plates, and side hustles directly.

Every transaction uses the mobile money and bank rails students already rely on every day: EcoCash, InnBucks, OneMoney, and ZIPIT — zero credit cards, zero friction, and safe on-campus meet-ups.
