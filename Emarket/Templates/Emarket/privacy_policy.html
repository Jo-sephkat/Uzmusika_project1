Privacy Policy

1. Campus Community Trust

UniTrade is exclusively for verified Zimbabwean tertiary students. We collect only essential contact info (student email and phone) necessary to facilitate peer-to-peer commerce and order notifications.

2. Direct Peer Payments

Payments occur directly between student accounts through mobile money operators (EcoCash, InnBucks, OneMoney, Banks). UniTrade does not store payment passwords or card PIN numbers.

3. Data Protection

Your account information is stored securely and never sold to third parties. If you wish to delete your account or listings, you can do so anytime from your profile dashboard.
